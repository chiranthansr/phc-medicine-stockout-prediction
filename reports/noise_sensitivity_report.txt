SYNTHETIC DEMAND NOISE SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS
===========================================

The same Z scores are used across scenarios; the current sigma=0.19 scenario should reproduce generated demand.
The 0.45 lower noise floor is retained in each scenario. This is demand-only and does not recalculate inventory or stock-outs.
These are illustrative assumptions, not empirical calibration to real PHC demand.
