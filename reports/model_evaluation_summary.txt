Per-medicine test metrics are in test_metrics_by_medicine.csv.
Pooled MAE/RMSE/R2 mix units (tablets/capsules/sachets) and must be interpreted cautiously. Macro normalized MAE computes MAE/actual-mean per medicine and averages these percentages, giving each medicine equal weight.
The target-day crisis flag is a scenario input. Crisis generalization cannot be validated from the final test split because its target dates contain no crisis period. A sensitivity check found demand-forecaster response to the crisis flag is not consistently upward for all medicines.

A separate next-day stock-out classifier and precision/recall/F1 evaluation are included in stockout_classifier_test_metrics.csv and stockout_classifier_evaluation.txt. These results are also synthetic-only and the test contains 14 positive labels.
